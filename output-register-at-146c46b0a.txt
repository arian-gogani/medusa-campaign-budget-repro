PART 4  ADJACENT OBSERVATION, a different defect, printed for honesty
==============================================================================
`max_quantity` under allocation EACH is applied within one line item.
Three lines of quantity 1 and one line of quantity 3 are the same goods
to a customer and not the same discount to the compute path.

No campaign budget at all on the promotion, so no budget guard runs:
  3 lines, qty 1, $100 each    -> discount=150    adjustments=3
  1 line,  qty 3, $300         -> discount=50     adjustments=1
  1 line,  qty 1, $300         -> discount=150    adjustments=1

That spread is not the budget defect and is not claimed as part of it.
It is printed because it is the reason the PART 1 pair uses a quantity-1
line of $300 rather than a quantity-3 line, and because it needs its own
pass before anything is said about it.
